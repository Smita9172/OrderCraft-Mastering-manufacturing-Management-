Modular ERP Architecture

Complete Manufacturing Operations Suite

OrderCraft brings every phase of discrete production, inventory control, and financial settlement into unified harmony.

Dashboard & Analytics

View business performance, sales, orders and important statistics in one place. Understand business progress through charts and reports.

Order Management

Create and manage customer orders. Track order status, priority, technical specifications, and delivery progress from booking to dispatch.

Product Management

Manage finished products and product information. Keep product details, selling prices, lead times, and manufacturing margins organized.

Bill of Materials (BOM)

Define raw materials required to manufacture each product. Understand product-wise material requirements with wastage and cost calculation.

Inventory Management

Monitor available raw materials and stock levels. Keep track of inventory, reorder thresholds, and warehouse rack/bay allocations.

Procurement Management

Manage vendors and purchase orders. Track material purchasing activities, delivery schedules, and Goods Receipt Notes (GRN).

Invoice Management

Generate and manage customer invoices. Track pending and completed invoices with full CGST/SGST breakdown and print-ready tax sheets.

Payment Management

Record customer payments. Monitor paid and pending amounts with automated real-time invoice balance reconciliation.

Customer Management

Store and manage customer information. Access customer-related order details, billing addresses, GSTIN numbers, and contact history.

Vendor Management

Maintain vendor information. Manage supplier relationships, evaluate quality ratings, and monitor agreed credit payment terms.

Reports & Analytics

View useful business insights and performance reports. Support better business decisions with 1-click CSV exports for compliance.

Manufacturing Lifecycle

How OrderCraft Works

A streamlined 7-step digital pipeline connecting client requirements to factory floor execution and financial clearance.

1

Add Customers and Vendors

Register client organizations with shipping details and approved suppliers with credit terms.

2

Create Products and Define Their BOM

Configure finished goods recipes with raw material specifications, scrap ratios, and unit costs.

3

Manage Raw Material Inventory

Track physical stock across warehouse bays with automated alerts for reorder thresholds.

4

Create Customer Orders

Book sales orders with required delivery deadlines, priority tags, and custom technical requirements.

5

Purchase Required Raw Materials

Run BOM shortage explosion, issue purchase orders, and receive consignments via GRN into stock.

6

Generate Invoices and Record Payments

Convert dispatched orders into GST tax invoices and reconcile customer settlements in real time.

7

Monitor Everything Through the Dashboard

Gain total transparency over plant capacity, active order pipeline, cashflow, and audit activity.
